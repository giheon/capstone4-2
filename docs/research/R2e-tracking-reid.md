## Caveats

- **Licensing fit for PrivFrame:** Apache/MIT + CPU path = roboflow/trackers (BoT-SORT/C-BIoU/OC-SORT) or supervision ByteTrack, plus OSNet exported to ONNX via torchreid `tools/export.py`, or FastReID SBS-R50 via `onnx_export.py`. BoxMOT and Ultralytics are AGPL-3.0; StrongSORT GPL-3.0; SAM2Long CC-BY-NC; KPR Hippocratic (restricts law-enforcement/military/mass-surveillance use; not OSI-approved).
- **Benchmarks are not comparable across rows:** BoxMOT's table is its own MOT17-ablation split; roboflow/trackers' table is its own harness with default params (HOTA only); official numbers use YOLOX-X on the MOT17 test server.
- **SAM2 family are SOT/VOS, GPU-bound**, no CPU path; SAM2MOT is the only MOT one and reports no MOT17.
- **Domain shift (primary-source evidence):** torchreid cross-domain table: osnet_x1_0 trained on MSMT17 → Market R1 66.6 / mAP 37.5 vs 94.2 / 82.6 same-domain; osnet_ain_x1_0 70.1 / 43.3 ([MODEL_ZOO](https://kaiyangzhou.github.io/deep-person-reid/MODEL_ZOO.html)). arXiv 2601.20598 (Jan 2026) reports supervised models "crumble on cross-domain data" and language-aligned models are more robust, but its protocol is unusual (OSNet 83.57 mAP Market vs 3.37 MSMT17) — treat as weak evidence ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20598v1)). KPR lists "cross-domain weights" as planned only. No source reports vlog/office-specific results.
- **UNVERIFIED:** ImprAsso code/license/numbers (CVF 403, no repo); CLIP-ReID/KPR/HAP param counts; SOLIDER/CLIP-ReID/TransReID weight licenses (none stated); SambaMOTR MOT17; CION small-model weights remain license-gated despite Apache code.
