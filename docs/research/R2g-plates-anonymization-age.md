## Caveats and recommendations

- **Plate masking needs detection only.** Best permissive CPU/ONNX fits for PrivFrame: open-image-models YOLOv9 (MIT) or the Apache RT-DETRv2 ONNX model; none report Korean-plate metrics and open-image-models' training set is undisclosed, so validate on Korean dashcam frames. fast-plate-ocr's global alphabet has no Hangul; Korean OCR would need KR_LPR_Jax (GPL) or your own CRNN trained on Usmankhujaev synthetic + AI Hub data.
- **AI Hub** datasets are "내국인만", approval-gated, training-use-only, non-redistributable; overseas use needs a separate NIA agreement.
- **EgoBlur** is Apache-2.0 and the strongest documented detector, but is Faster R-CNN (~400 MB, 1.5 s/2 MP on CPU), TorchScript not ONNX, email-gated download, and Meta explicitly discourages macOS.
- **License traps:** DashcamCleaner is really AGPL-3.0; most YOLOv8/11 repos pull Ultralytics (AGPL); InsightFace and MiVOLO-v2 (HF) and AdamCodd are non-commercial; MiVOLO repo vs HF licenses conflict — treat as non-commercial for the v2 weights.
- **Minor estimation:** no permissively licensed binary child/adult face classifier with independent evaluation exists; SigLIP2 (Apache) and faceage-onnx (Apache + DINOv3 terms) are the closest, both self-reported. 2602.07815 shows even strong specialized models misclassify minors as adults 39–100% at an 18 threshold; expect worse near 14. Keep age as a soft signal for conservative masking, not a legal determination.
- **UNVERIFIED:** Roboflow Korean datasets (HTTP 403), keremberke models (HTTP 401), "KoLPR/KorLPR", "AgeCLIP" (no such repos found; only andreasjansson/clip-age-predictor), DashcamCleaner training set, deface fps claims (none published).
