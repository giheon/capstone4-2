## Caveats and UNVERIFIED items

- **Your current stack is license-mixed:** YuNet is clean MIT, but w600k_r50/buffalo_l/antelopev2 and SCRFD weights are all "non-commercial research purposes only". Cleanest drop-in replacements for grouping: **SFace (Apache-2.0, ONNX, already in OpenCV)**, **AdaFace (MIT, PyTorch)**, **facenet-pytorch (MIT)**, **dlib (CC0 model)**. EdgeFace looks permissive on GitHub but its published weights are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
- **Small faces:** no permissive repo reports a <40 px embedding benchmark, but AdaFace's README shows the drop: IJB-S Surveillance-to-Single Rank-1 71.35 (AdaFace R100 WebFace12M) vs 57.35 (ArcFace MS1MV2), and TinyFace Rank-1 72.29 / 68.21 (AdaFace WebFace12M / MS1MV2) / 63.68 (CurricularFace) — against LFW ≥99.8 for the same nets. TinyFace = 5,139 IDs, 169,403 images, "average 20x16 pixels" ([source](https://qmul-tinyface.github.io/)). Training on WebFace4M/12M helps more than architecture here; expect identity grouping to fail on far/profile faces regardless of model. YuNet is documented for faces "between around 10x10 to 300x300"; BlazeFace requires "Face bounding box sides ... at least 20% of the corresponding image sides" and excludes faces >2 m (short) / >5 m (full) — unsuitable for street/dashcam.
- **Latency is not comparable across rows:** OpenCV Zoo numbers are at 160x120 (YuNet) / 150x150 (SFace); SCRFD "Infer" is GPU. No primary source benchmarks Apple Silicon; measure locally at 720p/1080p.
- **2025–2026 permissive detectors:** none verified that beats SCRFD-2.5GF on Hard under MIT/Apache. All YOLOv8/11-face variants are GPL/AGPL-tainted (Ultralytics). yakhyo/retinaface-pytorch (MIT, ONNX, MNetV2 Hard 83.59 original-scale) is the strongest permissive option found; CenterFace (MIT, Hard 0.875 per its README) is the other. "EdgeFace-det" — no such project found.
- UNVERIFIED: GhostFaceNets params; SFace backbone; MagFace/ElasticFace accuracies (paper-only); yolo-face WIDER AP; BlazeFace WIDER AP; biubug6 ONNX script; serengil `retina-face` weight provenance; YuNet README's two differing AP sets.
